It was an absolute delight to have Jörg Schmiedmayer visit us in Singapore earlier last month! :) please enjoy the article documenting the seminar he gave at NTU SPMS, written by Taufiq Murtadho who has dedicated most of his PhD to working in close collaboration with the Atomchip group, from the perspective of a theorist: [“Does an Isolated Quantum System Relax? by Prof Jörg Schmiedmayer”](https://www.ntu.edu.sg/ias/news-events/news/detail/does-an-isolated-quantum-system-relax--by-prof-jorg-schmiedmayer).

Meanwhile, Jörg's love of nature and walks remind us that it's good to brave the tropical heat and step out for fresh air, every once in a while 😉
