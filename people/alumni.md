**Jeongrak Son** joined in.Q as its first PhD student in August 2021. He completed his PhD at NTU in September 2025, continued working with in.Q through April 2026, and began a postdoctoral position at Freie Universität Berlin in May 2026. His doctoral research studied how auxiliary systems can enhance quantum information-processing tasks, particularly in resource theories and quantum algorithms. Visit his [personal website](https://jeongrak-son.github.io/index.html).
